DECAY

Biography

Decay is a darker side of Krazy Records, bringing a Gothic type, gloomy, and fucked up atmosphere into the label. The music focuses more on dark moods, obsession, loneliness, lust, toxic emotions, and the kind of thoughts that usually stay buried. With haunting vocals, dark basslines, eerie melodies, and a cold late night feeling, Decay's sound takes inspiration from Gothic music, post punk, and darker alternative sounds while still keeping its own identity. Decay isn't about trying to make everything sound perfect. It's about making music that feels uncomfortable, mysterious, seductive, and real. Whether it's a slow, atmospheric track, or something more intense, every release is meant to feel like you're stepping into a darker world for a few minutes.
